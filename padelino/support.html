Getting started

What do I need?

An iPhone with iOS 18 or later. Scoring on the wrist, heart rate and hit counting need an Apple Watch with watchOS 11 or later. Without a Watch you can still log matches and see your stats on iPhone.

How do I score a match?

Open Padelino on your Apple Watch, choose the rules (1 or 3 sets, golden point, super tiebreak) and tap Start Match. After each point, tap the team that won it. Padelino counts games, sets and tiebreaks. Tapped the wrong team? Use the undo button. Turn the Digital Crown or swipe up to see heart rate, calories and hits.

What is Just Track?

Just Track records time, heart rate, calories and hits without scoring each point. When you end it, enter the games for each set on the Watch, or save without a score and add it later on your iPhone from the match.

How do I add players to a match from the Watch?

The Watch doesn't ask for names. Open the match on your iPhone and fill in the players and team names there. Set yourself as the default player in My Profile › Manage Players so your results and stats are shown from your side.

Common questions

The score doesn't show on my iPhone

Make sure the Padelino Watch app is installed (in the Watch app on your iPhone) and that your Watch is connected to your iPhone. The live score updates when both are in range. The final result is always delivered, even if your iPhone was away during the match; it arrives when the devices reconnect.

Why are my matches saved as Pickleball in Apple Health?

Apple Health has no padel workout type yet, so Padelino uses the closest one. You can stop saving workouts in Settings › Save Matches as Workouts; heart rate and calories still show in the app.

How are hits counted?

Your Watch's motion sensors detect each swing. If the count seems too high or too low, change Settings › Sensitivity (Low, Medium or High), or turn Hit Tracking off.

How are heart rate zones worked out?

Zones are based on your maximum heart rate: 220 minus your age from Apple Health, or 190 if Health doesn't know your age. If you know your real maximum, set it in Settings › Custom Max Heart Rate.

Can I move my matches to a new iPhone?

Yes, through your normal iPhone backup: restoring an iCloud or computer backup brings your matches with it. You can also keep a copy with Settings › Export as CSV or Export as JSON.

How do I delete my data?

Settings › Delete All Data removes every match and player. Workouts saved to Apple Health are managed in the Health app. See the Privacy Policy for everything Padelino stores.

Which languages are available?

English and Bosnian. Change it in Settings › Language.
